

# MarketingOps Kit

### Open-source AI workflows for creative strategy and marketing intelligence.

MarketingOps Kit is a collection of structured, reusable AI-agent skills designed to help marketers research, analyze, develop, and evaluate campaigns.

The project emphasizes strategic reasoning, commercial relevance, evidence quality, and reproducible workflows.

## Why MarketingOps Kit?

Marketing teams increasingly use AI to generate campaign ideas and analyze advertisements.

But generic prompts often produce:

- Unsupported consumer insights
- Invented campaign performance
- Weak strategic differentiation
- Recommendations disconnected from business goals
- Confident answers based on incomplete evidence

MarketingOps Kit addresses these problems through structured workflows, quality gates, templates, and documented manual tests.

## Quick Start

1. Open a skill's `SKILL.md` file.
2. Read its workflow and quality requirements.
3. Open the corresponding template.
4. Provide a business brief or advertisement source.
5. Ask your AI assistant to follow the workflow.
6. Review the output against the quality checklist.

These are instruction-based workflows, not executable software or automated integrations.

Compatibility depends on the AI assistant's ability to read files and follow instructions.

## Testing

Skills are evaluated manually against structured test briefs.

Evaluation considers:

- Input fidelity
- Source integrity
- Strategic reasoning
- Commercial logic
- Recommendation quality
- Experiment design

Initial tests have identified and helped address issues involving fabricated claims, unsupported scoring, and weak KPI alignment.

The tests are exploratory and do not establish production reliability.
