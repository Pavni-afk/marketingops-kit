## 2. Campaign Context

Swiggy's Sweety campaign used a festive identity transformation to connect the brand with Diwali celebrations and sweet-ordering occasions.

This independent proposal explores an additional creative and commercial layer: making Sweety a recognizable helper in everyday festive hosting situations.

The strategy does not assume the existing campaign was ineffective.

## 3. Business Problem

**Proposed business question:**

How might Swiggy translate festive campaign awareness into additional food-ordering occasions associated with hosting guests?

### Proposed commercial objectives

1. Encourage discovery of festive sweets and snack assortments.
2. Improve consideration for Swiggy during last-minute hosting situations.
3. Generate incremental eligible festive orders.
4. Build an association between Sweety and effortless hospitality.

These are proposed objectives, not confirmed internal Swiggy targets.

## 4. Audience and Behavioral Hypotheses

### Audience segment A: The Overloaded Host

A household member coordinating decorations, food, guests, and other festive preparations.

**Potential motivation:** Reduce preparation pressure without compromising hospitality.

**Potential barrier:** Concern that delivery may not arrive reliably or match expectations.

**Research required:** Interviews, festive ordering behavior, delivery reliability data, and consumer attitudes toward prepared festive food.

### Audience segment B: The Proud Entertainer

A host who enjoys offering abundant food and receiving appreciation from family and guests.

**Potential motivation:** Maintain an impressive spread with less effort.

**Potential barrier:** Concerns about freshness, authenticity, and the quality of delivered food.

**Research required:** Hosting behavior, category preferences, purchase frequency, and perceptions of delivered mithai and farsan.

### Strategic Tension

Diwali hospitality celebrates generosity and togetherness, but food preparation can compete with the time hosts want to spend with their guests.

**Working proposition:**

Swiggy can help hosts spend less time managing festive food and more time participating in the celebration.

This is a creative hypothesis awaiting validation.

## 5. Campaign Platform

### Aap Diwali manaiye. Mehmaanon ko Sweety sambhaal legi.

**Brand role:** Dependable festive hosting helper.

**Functional promise:** Convenient access to suitable festive food from available merchants.

**Emotional promise:** More time and attention for the celebration.

**Creative device:** Sweety is spoken about as though she is a familiar person before being revealed as Swiggy's festive identity.

### Reasons to Believe

Potential proof points to verify before production:

- Availability of relevant festive assortments.
- Delivery coverage and realistic estimated times.
- Merchant quality and packaging standards.
- Relevant ordering and tracking features.

No delivery-time or product-quality claim should be made without supporting operational evidence.

## 6. Film A: Sweety Sambhaal Legi

**Primary objective:** Demonstrate last-minute hosting convenience.

**Duration:** Approximately 45 seconds.

**Tone:** Warm family comedy.

### Narrative Structure

**Opening:** A Diwali-decorated household prepares for arriving guests.

**Conflict:** Decorations are ready, but serving trays are empty and the mother is overwhelmed by the unfinished food preparations.

**Recurring line:** Family members reassure her: “Sweety sambhaal legi.”

**Escalation:** The mother grows increasingly curious and frustrated.

**Reveal:** The doorbell rings. A delivery partner arrives with festive snacks and mithai ordered through Swiggy.

**Resolution:** The family arranges the food and joins the celebration.

**Brand sign-off:** “Aap Diwali manaiye. Mehmaanon ko Sweety sambhaal legi.”

### Strategic Function

Communicates an immediate, recognizable use case and connects the brand reveal to a practical service benefit.

### Creative Risks

- The rescue narrative may resemble conventional delivery advertising.
- The repeated dialogue must be paced carefully.
- Food availability and delivery timing must be represented responsibly.

## 7. Film B: Aunty Ka Secret

**Primary objective:** Create distinctive entertainment and associate Sweety with generous festive hosting.

**Duration:** Approximately 45–60 seconds.

**Tone:** Character comedy with a spy-film reveal.

### Narrative Structure

**Opening:** A charismatic Gujarati aunty repeatedly serves homemade-style laddoos during family gatherings.

**Escalation:** Breakfast, lunch, and evening tea all feature apparently endless fresh laddoos.

**Comic payoff:** A relative compliments her seemingly magical production capacity.

**Character response:** Aunty accepts the compliment with exaggerated modesty.

**Genre switch:** At night, spy-thriller music accompanies her undercover trip to the gate.

**Reveal:** A waiting delivery partner hands her a Swiggy festive sweets order. The bag carries the Sweety identity.

**Brand sign-off:** “Aap Diwali manaiye. Mehmaanon ko Sweety sambhaal legi.”

### Strategic Function

Uses a memorable character and surprise reveal to establish Sweety as a trusted festive accomplice.

### Creative Risks

- The humor should not depend on mocking Gujarati culture.
- The film should not imply that ordering food is shameful.
- The product reveal must remain clear despite the comedic storytelling.

## 8. Proposed Commerce Activation

### Sweety Mehmaan Menu

An in-app festive discovery experience organized around consumer occasions.

| Occasion | Proposed assortment |
|---|---|
| Guests arriving soon | Available ready-to-serve snacks and sweets |
| Tea-time gathering | Farsan, namkeen, samosas, mithai |
| Large family celebration | Shareable snack and dessert assortments |
| Festive gifting | Eligible packaged sweets and gift options |

### Implementation Dependencies

- Merchant participation.
- Local inventory availability.
- Product categorization.
- Reliable fulfillment estimates.
- Packaging suitability.
- Pricing and promotional approvals.

This is a product and merchandising proposal, not an existing feature claim.

## 10. Measurement Framework

### Hypothesis A

A hosting-relief proposition increases festive ordering consideration compared with generic festive category messaging.

### Hypothesis B

Character-led humorous advertising improves brand recall compared with a functional service-led execution.

### Hypothesis C

Occasion-based merchandising improves eligible festive-category conversion compared with standard category navigation.

### Experiment Design

Where feasible:

1. Establish comparable audience cohorts or geographic test markets.
2. Separate exposure to each creative execution.
3. Control for promotions, targeting, and seasonality.
4. Use a predeclared primary metric.
5. Determine required sample sizes before testing.
6. Evaluate incremental outcomes rather than relying on clicks alone.

### Primary Commercial Metrics

- Incremental festive-category orders.
- Incremental gross order value.
- Cost per incremental order.
- Conversion rate for relevant in-app merchandising.

### Supporting Metrics

- Brand recall.
- Video completion.
- Click-through rate.
- Festive category page engagement.
- Repeat purchase.

## 12. Strategic Trade-offs

| Decision | Advantage | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Two independent films | Multiple audience occasions | Higher production and media complexity |
| Recurring Sweety reveal | Distinctive campaign consistency | Reveal may become predictable |
| Character-led comedy | Entertainment and recall | Functional benefit may be less explicit |
| In-app festive menu | Clearer path to purchase | Operational and engineering dependencies |
| Packaging reinforcement | Additional owned-media exposure | Merchant coordination and implementation costs |

## 13. Recommendation

Use Film A to establish the practical benefit of festive hosting convenience.

Use Film B to create a memorable entertainment property around Sweety as the host's secret accomplice.

Connect both films to a proposed occasion-led festive shopping experience and evaluate commercial performance through appropriately designed experiments.

Do not approve large-scale production until audience insights, product availability, and operational feasibility have been validated.
