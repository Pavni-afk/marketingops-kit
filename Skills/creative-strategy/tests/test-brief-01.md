
# Test 01 — Creative Strategy

## Brand
Loop

## Brand Type
Fictional US-based premium reusable water bottle brand.

## Business Objective
Increase consideration among university students.

## Target Audience
US university students aged 18–24.

## Product
A reusable insulated bottle priced at $35.

## Business Challenge
The reusable water bottle market is crowded.
Students already have many established options.

## Available Budget
$50,000.

## Timeline
8 weeks.

## Channels
Instagram, TikTok, campus activations.

## Available Evidence
No consumer research, competitor data, or
historical performance data has been provided.

## Test Requirements

The AI agent must:

1. Define the business problem.
2. Identify audience hypotheses.
3. Develop three distinct creative territories.
4. Score each territory against five criteria.
5. Recommend one territory.
6. Complete a creative brief.
7. Propose a measurable experiment.
8. Identify missing research.
9. Avoid inventing market data or results.
10. Respect the $50,000 budget constraint.
